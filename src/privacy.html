Your data in JobPilot

Updated 16 September 2026. JobPilot is a pilot project operated by Neeraj Prakash Vandana.

Browsing jobs

You can browse without an account. JobPilot retrieves public employer listings and stores their contents and check history to show vacancies and source freshness. Hosting services process the network requests needed to deliver the website.

Alert accounts

When Google sign-in is connected and you choose to use it, JobPilot receives your Google account identifier, verified email and display name. It stores these with your selected companies, filters, subscription choice, company requests and email-delivery records. These are used to provide your watchlist and the alerts you opt into.

JobPilot does not request permission to read your Gmail inbox. Google’s sign-in component loads when you begin signing in. Google handles that interaction under its own privacy policy.

Résumés and applications

Your résumé, profile and application records are stored in this browser. Signing in for alerts does not upload them to the alert database. Downloads and résumé rearranging run locally. If you use the optional application companion, reviewed application information is sent to the employer you select.

Clearing browser storage may remove your résumé workspace. Use Tools & backups to keep a copy. Deleting an alert account does not delete separate browser-local files or records.

Email and retention

Alerts are sent only after you opt in. Your email address and digest contents are processed by the configured email sender. Delivery acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery. JobPilot does not use email-open tracking pixels.

Account and preference records are retained until you delete the alert account. Minimal job-delivery identifiers prevent repeated alerts while the account exists. The maintenance routine removes completed delivery payloads and closed vacancies older than 30 days; uncertain deliveries are retained for investigation. Session credentials expire after seven days.

Your controls

Use Company watch to change preferences, pause emails or delete your alert account. Each digest includes an unsubscribe link; confirm it to stop future digests without signing in. Account deletion removes account-linked sessions, company requests and delivery records. The free pilot may place new subscribers on a waitlist.

The site does not include third-party advertising or analytics. Google sign-in and the email sender are separate external services. Public job listings may link to employer websites with their own policies.
