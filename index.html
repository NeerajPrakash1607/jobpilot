Seven employer boards — Stripe, Intercom, Datadog, Squarespace, MongoDB, Toast, and Reddit — plus the Remotive public remote jobs feed. This is a selection of sources, not every vacancy online.

Employer feeds refresh on search after 30 minutes; Remotive after 6 hours and its public listings are delayed by 24 hours. Remote matches use the advertised region; check the listing’s residency and work requirements. Skill overlap is a keyword comparison, not an assessment of your eligibility.

Checking the last companion connection…

Prepare a role, open its application form, and approve it in the browser companion. It can fill your details, attach your résumé, submit supported forms, and record the confirmation.

  1. Download the browser extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Select the unzipped folder containing manifest.json.
  3. Click the JobPilot extension icon and paste the pairing key below. The local address is http://127.0.0.1:5181.
  4. On the employer’s application form, select your prepared role, review the page check, and choose Fill application for review. Finish any unanswered questions on the employer page. To send it, confirm the role and your reviewed answers, then click Auto-apply this role.

The companion runs on the tab where you click it. Missing answers, CAPTCHA, agreements and unsupported forms pause automation. If confirmation is unclear, retries are blocked until you verify the outcome. Version 2.3.1 supports standard forms and recognised Ashby forms. On Ashby, choose location suggestions yourself and review them before approving. If already installed, update its files and reload the companion in your browser’s Extensions page after updating it.

YOUR DATA, KEPT CLOSE

A copy for peace of mind.

Your profile, résumé, applications, and confirmation notes live in a database on this computer. Both localhost addresses use the same data.

Automatic snapshots are kept at startup and before the first change each day. Restoring creates a recovery snapshot first. The latest 14 snapshots are retained in data/backups.
